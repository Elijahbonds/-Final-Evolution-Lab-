# Clinical Logic Documentation: Vertical Velocity Academy (VVA)

## 1. NMS Mapping & SFMA/FMS Integration
The VVA curriculum is not just a training program; it is a **Neuromuscular Correction System (NMS)**. Each of the 10 modules is programmatically linked to specific **SFMA (Selective Functional Movement Assessment)** and **FMS (Functional Movement Screen)** fail-states.

### Logic Flow:
1. **Assessment:** Athlete performs FMS/SFMA screening.
2. **Detection:** System identifies a "Fail" (Score < 2 or asymmetry).
3. **Unlock:** The corresponding VVA module is automatically unlocked in the athlete's dashboard.
   - *Example:* A fail in the **Deep Squat** (indicating restricted ankle dorsiflexion) triggers the **Ankle Piston** module to restore reactive stiffness.

## 2. The Bonds Standard (Ground Contact Time)
The **Bonds Standard** is the clinical benchmark for elite explosive output.
- **Metric:** Ground Contact Time (GCT).
- **Benchmark:** < 0.100s (100ms).
- **Clinical Significance:** GCT is the primary predictor of vertical displacement. The shorter the GCT, the higher the rate of force development (RFD).

## 3. Penultimate Stride & Haptic Feedback
The penultimate stride is the "long-short" rhythm required to convert horizontal velocity into vertical lift.
- **The Error:** "Energy Leakage" caused by an over-extended or under-loaded penultimate step.
- **The Correction:** The Sovereign app uses real-time **Haptic Feedback** (via wearable integration or mobile vibration) to signal the exact millisecond of foot strike.
- **Mechanism:** If the GCT exceeds the Bonds Standard or the stride length deviates from the athlete's optimal biomechanical model, a sharp haptic pulse is delivered to the athlete's device, forcing a neuromuscular correction in the subsequent rep.

## 4. Shard Economy (Cost-to-Output)
The Shard economy is designed to incentivize high-frequency, high-quality data collection.
- **100 Shards = 1 Neural Drive Scan:** A focused analysis of the athlete's central nervous system (CNS) readiness and RFD.
- **500 Shards = 1 Apex Evaluation:** A comprehensive, multi-vector analysis of the athlete's peak jump performance, including takeoff angle, GCT, and flight time.
