1. **The Ankle Piston (Reactive Stiffness)**:
   - Derived from: 10-second finger tap speed and POGO test.
   - Logic: High stiffness translates to lower ground contact time and faster reactive force.
   - Variable: `anklePistonStiffness` (0.5 - 2.0).

2. **The Hip Hinge (Explosive Power)**:
   - Derived from: PRQ (Performance Readiness Quotient).
   - Formula: `JumpHeight = (PRQ / 100) * 1.5 + (Stiffness * 0.5)`.
   - Variable: `hipHingeExplosion`.

3. **Neural Drive**:
   - The "Battery" of the athlete.
   - High Neural Drive increases acceleration and reduces "Neural Friction" (input lag).
